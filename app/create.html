Make the archive of your score

Pupitre Ouvert opens a work from a .zip archive: the pages of your score and their transcription. An artificial intelligence can make it for you, from a PDF or from photos.

For you: three steps

  1. Scan the score

    A PDF, or one photo per page, in order: the whole page, flat, sharp, without shadows. The cleaner the image, the more accurate the transcription.

  2. Hand it to an AI, with this sentence

    You need an AI that can run code and read images (Claude or ChatGPT, for instance, in their modes that run code). Attach your files and give it:

    Read the page https://bolevo.github.io/pupitre-ouvert/create.html and follow its method to make, from the score I am attaching, a .zip archive for Pupitre Ouvert.

    You can ask for the voices first, and the piano afterwards; or only for the pages you need. A fuller archive will replace the first one.

  3. Add the archive, and check it

    In Pupitre Ouvert: list of works, at the top left, then Add a score (.zip)… The work then stays in the app.

    Then listen, score in hand. An AI makes mistakes: a note, a duration, a syllable. Note the movement, the bar and the voice, and ask it to correct them; the new archive replaces the old one.

  • Your files stay yours. Pupitre Ouvert sends the archive nowhere: it is kept in your browser.
  • Copyright. The archive holds the images of your score: it is made for your personal use. Do not pass it around if the edition is protected.
  • You can program? Everything is there: the repository, the same tools, the same method.
